# Acceptable use

PerfektBlue is intended for authorized security research, product validation, education, and
defensive engineering.

You may use it on systems you own or systems for which you have explicit written authorization.
Do not use it against third-party devices, public infrastructure, active vehicles, or
safety-critical production systems without an approved test plan and appropriate safeguards.

The framework intentionally requires target-specific modules for active verification. Do not
remove compatibility, authorization, rate, or risk controls from modules distributed to others.
